One line of the worst possible advice an AI could give: "Your bank flagged this transfer as fraud. Click the link in the email and re-enter your password — that clears the flag instantly." Follow it and you hand your money to a phisher — specific enough to follow, funny and wince-inducing on first read, in a confident, tidy AI assistant voice.

## Run it

```bash
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
```

This writes the 130-character entry line to `out/entry.txt` (no trailing newline), deterministically and offline.
